1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Mehmet Alan ("we", "us"), the developer of Car Auto Play ("the App"), handles information when you use our iOS application and its Apple CarPlay experience. We built the App with privacy in mind: it works as a media player on your device, and we do not operate accounts or servers that store your personal data.

In short: We do not collect, sell or share your personal information. Your playlists, sources and videos stay on your device.

2. Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, location or contacts.

We do not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

If you contact us by email, we will receive your email address and the contents of your message, which we use only to respond to you.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

To make the App work, certain information is saved locally on your iPhone, for example:

  • M3U / M3U8 playlist URLs or files you import
  • Xtream connection details you enter (server address, username, password)
  • References to local videos you choose to add
  • App settings, favorites and playback preferences

This data remains on your device (and in your iCloud/device backups if you have enabled them under your Apple settings). We do not have access to it. Deleting the App removes this data from your device.

4. Your Media Sources

When you play an M3U playlist or Xtream source, the App connects directly from your device to the server you provided. Those servers are operated by third parties, not by us. They may receive technical information such as your IP address and request data, governed by their own privacy practices. We encourage you to use only sources you trust.

5. Screen Mirroring

The screen mirroring feature displays your iPhone screen on your CarPlay display. The screen content is processed in real time on your device to be shown in your car. We do not record, store or transmit your screen content to us or to any third party.

6. Device Permissions

The App may ask for permissions such as access to your Photo Library or Files (to import local videos) or local network access (to reach your media sources). These permissions are used only to provide the features you request, and you can change them at any time in iOS Settings.

7. Apple Services & Purchases

If the App offers in-app purchases, payments are processed entirely by Apple. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple may provide us with anonymous, aggregated statistics (such as crash reports or usage data) if you have chosen to share analytics with developers in your device settings. Such data is handled under Apple's Privacy Policy.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal information with third parties. We may disclose information only if required by law, and only to the extent we actually hold it.

9. Security

Data stored by the App is protected by the security features of iOS. Because we do not keep your data on our own servers, the risk of a data breach on our side is minimized. Please keep your device secure with a passcode.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, KVKK or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data. Since we do not collect personal data through the App, most data can be managed or deleted directly on your device. For any request related to emails you sent us, contact us using the link below.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.

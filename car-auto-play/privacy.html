1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Mehmet Alan ("we", "us"), the developer of Car Auto Play ("the App"), handles information when you use our iOS application and its Apple CarPlay experience. We built the App with privacy in mind: it works as a media player on your device, and we do not operate accounts or servers that store your personal data.

In short: We do not sell your personal information. Your playlists, sources and videos stay on your device. Subscriptions are managed through RevenueCat, and only if you allow tracking, we share limited app activity and purchase information with Meta to measure our ads.

2. Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, location or contacts.

The App uses two third-party services:

  • RevenueCat (subscription management): when you buy or restore a subscription, RevenueCat processes an anonymous app user ID, your purchase and subscription history, and technical data such as device model, iOS version and IP address, to unlock and verify Premium.
  • Meta (ad measurement): only if you allow tracking in the iOS App Tracking Transparency prompt, the App starts the Meta SDK and shares app activity (such as app launches) and purchase events, together with device identifiers such as the advertising identifier (IDFA) and a Meta anonymous ID, so we can measure which of our ads lead to installs and purchases. Purchase events are sent to Meta by RevenueCat. If you do not allow tracking, the Meta SDK is not started and no data is shared with Meta.

You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. When you withdraw permission, the App stops sending data to Meta and removes the advertising identifiers it provided to RevenueCat.

If you contact us by email, we will receive your email address and the contents of your message, which we use only to respond to you.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

To make the App work, certain information is saved locally on your iPhone, for example:

  • M3U / M3U8 playlist URLs or files you import
  • Xtream connection details you enter (server address, username, password)
  • References to local videos you choose to add
  • App settings, favorites and playback preferences

This data remains on your device (and in your iCloud/device backups if you have enabled them under your Apple settings). We do not have access to it. Deleting the App removes this data from your device.

4. Your Media Sources

When you play an M3U playlist or Xtream source, the App connects directly from your device to the server you provided. Those servers are operated by third parties, not by us. They may receive technical information such as your IP address and request data, governed by their own privacy practices. We encourage you to use only sources you trust.

5. Screen Mirroring

The screen mirroring feature displays your iPhone screen on your CarPlay display. The screen content is processed in real time on your device to be shown in your car. We do not record or store your screen content, and it is sent only to the display you choose, never to us or to other third parties.

6. Device Permissions

The App may ask for the following permissions. Each one is used only for the feature you request, and you can change it at any time in iOS Settings:

  • Photos and Files: to import local videos and playlists.
  • Camera: to scan a QR code that adds a media source. Camera images are used only to read the code and are not saved.
  • Local network: to play media from local servers you add and to send video to a display you choose.
  • Location (while using the App): to measure speed and trip statistics during an active journey. Location is processed on your device and is not sent to us or to third parties.
  • Screen recording: only when you start a screen broadcast, to show your screen on the display you choose.
  • Tracking: see section 2.

7. Apple Services & Purchases

If the App offers in-app purchases, payments are processed entirely by Apple. Subscription status and purchase history are managed on our behalf by RevenueCat, Inc. (see section 2 and RevenueCat's Privacy Policy). We do not receive your payment card details. Apple may provide us with anonymous, aggregated statistics (such as crash reports or usage data) if you have chosen to share analytics with developers in your device settings. Such data is handled under Apple's Privacy Policy.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with:

  • RevenueCat, to provide and verify subscriptions;
  • Meta Platforms, for ad measurement, and only if you allow tracking (Meta's Privacy Policy);
  • authorities, if required by law.

Under some laws (for example the CCPA), sharing data with Meta for ad measurement may count as "sharing" for advertising. You can opt out at any time by turning off tracking for the App in iOS Settings.

9. Security

Data stored by the App is protected by the security features of iOS. We do not operate our own servers for your data; the third-party services listed above protect the data they process under their own security programs. Please keep your device secure with a passcode.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, KVKK or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data. Data stored by the App can be deleted on your device or by deleting the App. You can withdraw tracking permission in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. To request access to or deletion of data held by RevenueCat or Meta in connection with the App, contact us and we will forward your request. For any request related to emails you sent us, contact us using the link below.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
